Privacy policy

QT-FM World archive client. Effective 16 November 2026.

Purpose and access

This personal integration lets its owner archive and restore selected QT-FM World research and operational files using Google Drive. It requests the Google Drive drive.file scope. It can access files created through this integration or explicitly made available to the app; it does not request access to all files in the owner's Drive.

Data handling

The laptop transfers designated archive files, filenames, checksums and related file metadata to Google Drive. Google hosts the remote copies under the owner's account. Operational archives can include research outputs and service records. Authentication credentials are kept in an owner-protected local directory. Any credential material included in remote recovery archives must be encrypted before upload. API calls use HTTPS.

Use and sharing

The archive integration uses Google API data for storage, integrity verification and restoration. It does not sell that data, use it for advertising, send Drive file contents to outside AI services, or use those contents to train general-purpose AI models. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and retention

The owner controls local and remote retention. Archive files remain until the owner removes them. The owner can revoke the app's Google account access at any time through Google's account permissions settings; revocation does not itself delete previously stored local or remote archives.

Contact

For questions or deletion assistance, contact the operator through the public support issue tracker. Do not include secrets or private files in public issues.
